What we do

Six jobs that keep the fleet on the road

Every task comes through the Lime operations app. You reserve it, go to the bike, do the job and mark it done. These are the six tasks you learn with us.

  • Swap

    Charged batteries in, empty ones out

    Take charged batteries from our charging rack and swap them into bikes that are running low.

    Bicycle · van
  • Service

    Tighten the brakes

    Small on-street fixes with a screwdriver, so every rider gets a bike that stops safely.

    Any vehicle
  • Tidy

    Same bikes, better street

    Stand fallen bikes up and line them up in their spot so footpaths stay clear.

    Any vehicle
  • Move

    Right bike, wrong place

    Take bikes from where they pile up to the spots where people are looking for one.

    Van
  • Retrieve

    Broken bikes go back to the warehouse

    Collect bikes that can't be ridden and bring them in for repair.

    Van
  • Deploy

    From the warehouse to the street

    Load ready bikes from the warehouse and put them out where they're needed.

    Van

How to join

From application to your first shift

Most of it happens on your phone. You visit us once, and we start you off alongside an experienced rider.

  1. Apply online

    Answer a few quick questions about your work rights, vehicle and availability. It takes about two minutes.

  2. Do the online course

    Learn the six tasks with short animated lessons, in English or Spanish, and pass the final quiz.

  3. Upload your documents

    Passport, visa or VEVO, proof of address and a photo. We check them before we meet you.

  4. Book your session

    Pick a Monday or Tuesday that suits you. Sessions start at 11:00 am.

    Lucky Bikes, Ultimo

  5. Meet us in person

    We set up your Juicers app account, give you your hi-vis gear and practise the tasks on real bikes.

  6. Start working

    Your first shift is alongside an experienced rider. After that you add your ABN and bank details and you're on the team.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need experience?

No. You learn every task in our online course, then practise them with us in person before your first shift.

How do I get paid?

You work as an independent contractor and we pay you to your ABN for the tasks you complete.

I don't have an ABN. Can I still apply?

Yes. Applying for an ABN is free on the Australian Business Register (abr.gov.au). Start it while you do the course so it's ready by your first shift.

Can students apply?

Yes. Students need to commit to at least 20 hours a week.

What vehicle do I need?

Your own van, bicycle or motorbike. Tidy and Service work with all three. Swap needs a bicycle or a van. Move, Retrieve and Deploy carry whole bikes, so they need a van.

Is the course in English?

You can take it in English or Spanish. The Lime operations app is in English, so we show you every button by its English name.

What do I get at the in-person session?

Your Juicers app account, your hi-vis safety gear and hands-on practice with the tasks on real bikes.
